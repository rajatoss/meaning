Waterfall
November 2026

I saw my first waterfall three months back. I have seen rivers, seashores, and some sort of hills, and most of these seem to have a specific sound. Sounds rarely help you with concentration: if it's a crowd, it's mostly irritating, and if it is some sort of art, i.e. music, then it is mostly an amplification of an emotion that I am already going through. Neither is particularly helpful when you're trying to make a decision, but water appears to have this figured out. It is able to create sounds which are almost always calming, rain hitting a surface, waves rising and then dissolving on their own, or the sound of a splash on my face.

I have seen pretty things. I have seen a cat and a dog, I have seen a leaf up close after the rain, and a rainbow in a puddle. I also saw her a few days back, and the mole on her neck. I also saw the sunset last night, and a sunrise a year ago. I saw my mother's eyes and my father's hands. I saw a PSLV, and I also saw BrahMos when I was in school.

These aren't ordinary things.

Every time I saw them, I felt some sort of love. I thought of capturing them, writing about them, or storing them in my eyes. I felt a sense of intrinsic drive to make something beautiful, and halfway through looking at them, I felt the fear of ending. I saw the power of time. I didn't like something this powerful and with this much control, so I tried to stay a little longer. I said hii to the cat twice and acted scared of the dog for two more minutes, but I couldn't entertain the sun, or be with her a little longer. The sun was too big for me, and she had work early in the morning. Dad was on a call and Mom was sleepy, they were closing the museum, and the last bus from the Air Force station to home had just arrived.

The world was constantly changing, it was up to me to hold it, and I weigh only 60 kgs and the world was too heavy, so it started to become a reflection of my inabilities. They said change is what makes the world beautiful, and happiness should be given in small doses, and love, unlike greatness, can't be earned. Acceptance has a sound similar to losing and I pretty much hate it. With sound, I still remember the waterfall. I never thought falling could be this beautiful, but Mom said twice this morning that beauty lies in the eyes of the beholder. Basically, she called me pretty twice.

And all that love is in me.
