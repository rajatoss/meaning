Two
September 2026

Diet Coke is a weird drink. Also, it's raining outside. All my washed clothes are completely soaked in water. Well, talking about water, I think I am also a little thirsty, but I also want to pee. The chair is too comfortable, and I am sitting with my legs on the wall. The wall has a crack, and if I give enough time to my imagination, I can paint a picture. Well, I wore new clothes today. I should have taken a real picture, but real and imaginary are more of a construct. No, I don't agree with that. Going to the office is real, while thinking I will take a sick leave tomorrow morning is imagination. Thinking burns a lot of calories. Ohh, these calories, man. Now I remember that I did not go to the gym today. What did I do then? Ohh, I did the laundry, and it's raining outside.

There is a turtle near my window. It seems to be very slow. Well, unlike me, he is not on a chair and is making progress.

Well, on progress, I have been told slow is fake, and life is short. But a friend told me turtles live for like 400 years. 400 is a lot. By lot, I mean the dal I made today in the afternoon. I could eat it at night too, so a lot is when you can eat it twice. Twice is what you get when you double something in half the time. Sorry, that's 4 times. Maybe 4 times something is actually enough, but people on the internet said that it's never enough.

Never is also a synonym for impossible. Coming to synonyms, I would like to add “love” to the list too. Is two times 1 a synonym for twice? Mom said there are no dumb questions, although she agrees this one is a dumb one. Not sure if she is talking about the question or me. Well, “not sure” is for sure a synonym for uncertainty, and the turtle is certainly making progress, while I am uncertain about mine. But nothing in the world is mine, like those clothes that are now dipped in rain. I have abandoned them for tonight, and it is also almost 2 in the night.

Two is my favorite number. Well, to be honest, I think 4 is better. Better, but not enough.
